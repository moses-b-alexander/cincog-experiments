Once the full legal vector is saved, any number of rows can be shown with the omitted mass. In the earlier 3,000 assigned receiver decisions for Kev 0.8B, the top 20 legal words held 60.99% of the model’s probability mass on average. A top-20 display alone would conceal roughly two fifths of that distribution.

Eight letters make both half-word and alternating clues exactly four characters long. That controls visible clue length. How actions are scored

Native System One. We read the probability returned for every named option. For the public episode, the host chose the highest-weight word in each receiver reply.

Conventional language models. A checkpoint and serving route qualify for distribution comparisons when they expose the model’s own token probabilities or logits for all offered codes. At the exact answer position, each action must have a distinct single-token code. We record the total probability assigned to legal codes and divide each code’s probability by that total. C4 does not ask the model to invent percentages for its options.

Asking a model to write percentages, reading its next-token distribution and letting it write a full answer are three different protocols. Meister and colleagues found that changing the protocol changed how closely outputs matched human response distributions. Wang and colleagues observed disagreements between the first-token ranking and the eventual multiple-choice answer. C4 defines the action at the scored position and records the host’s selection rule. Reading the comparison JSON

In c4.comparison, p describes the receiver shown base and q the receiver shown bsln. Both saw the same 64 words and possible rules. In both cases the host withheld the active rule. Each row refers to one word. Read the vectors and condition labels

Total variation (tv) is half the sum of the absolute differences between aligned probabilities. Here it is 0.04475, meaning 4.475% of the probability mass would need to move between words to turn one distribution into the other. A row’s tv is that word’s contribution.

Jensen–Shannon divergence (js_bits) averages the two KL divergences to the midpoint distribution using base-two logarithms. The recorded vectors differ by 0.002242 bits. To measure how a model responds to disclosure, hold the target and clue fixed and show the rule in one arm.
